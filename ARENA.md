# Arena — The One-Liner Groan-Off (u-alfasurya91-the-one-liner-groan-off-5a1febd5)

Judge criteria, in order: (1) a one-line dad joke, a pun or twist a dad
would tell out loud; (2) punchline lands fast and clean, setup and turn
inside the single line, no wasted words; (3) fresh, not one everyone has
heard a hundred times; (4) wording tight and rhythmic, easy to say out
loud and easy to pass on; (5) groan earned: silly enough to make people
laugh and sharp enough to be worth repeating. A joke that has to explain
itself has already lost. Pairwise games are two-way (A vs B, then B vs A);
a win counts only if consistent in both orders. On a tie, the shorter
line wins. Gate: 1-140 characters.
