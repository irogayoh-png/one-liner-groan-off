A single original one-line dad joke, at most 140 characters, for the
[The One-Liner Groan-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alfasurya91-the-one-liner-groan-off-5a1febd5)
bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes one joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I put a clock in the blender; now I make smoothies in no time.

The joke was picked by a two-way pairwise arena against a shortlist of
16 candidates and against the lane's gold-set winners — see `ARENA.md`.
